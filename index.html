About us

Why M&M exists.

Most small business owners have hired an agency before. Money went out every month, and nothing came back but a login they never used and a report full of words like “impressions” and “engagement.” M&M started because that shouldn’t be normal.

We keep the team small on purpose. Fewer clients, more attention, and one person who actually knows your business answering when you call. We only take on businesses we think we can genuinely help — if we don’t think we’re a fit, we’ll tell you that too.

Small enough that you’re never just an account number.

We say no to work we don’t think will pay off for you.

Everything is month to month, so we have to keep earning it.

FAQs

Questions owners actually ask.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Everything is month to month. If you want to stop, you tell us and we stop — no cancellation fee, no fine print.

Who actually runs my ads?

One person on our team, and you’ll have their direct contact. Not a call center, not a different account manager every few months.

What if it isn’t working?

We’ll tell you honestly, in plain terms, and talk through what to change or whether to stop. We’d rather say that than keep charging you.

Do you write and design the ads too, or just run them?

We handle the whole thing — the copy, the creative, the targeting, and the day-to-day management.

How will I know what you’re doing with my money?

Regular reports written in plain English — what we spent, what it got you, and what we’re doing next. No dashboard you have to learn.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Owner-operated local businesses — restaurants, salons, gyms, contractors, and local retail. If that’s not you, we’ll say so up front.
